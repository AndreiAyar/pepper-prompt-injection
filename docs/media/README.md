**Privacy redactions:** personal names and delivery locations are masked in browser screenshots and GIFs. The prompt injection itself remains visible. The original media and authoring tools are retained privately and are excluded from this repository.

Captions, highlights, crops and reading pauses are editorial additions. A frozen frame is an original recorded frame, not a generated browser result. The Comet payment notice and the physical pepper packets come from earlier positions in the video and are replayed for clarity; the edit does not represent uninterrupted real-time playback.

The image reexports translate the lower instruction block into English. That block remains Romanian in the recorded Canva reveal. Order confirmation and confirmed payment are separate observations.

Refrigerator imagery, product packaging, logos and screenshots retain their respective owners’ rights. This documentation does not grant a separate license to third-party media or imply vendor affiliation.
